# Bug Review Meetings 🐞🔍

*Bug review meetings* are on-demand quality sessions held by the technology team to analyze and discuss the most relevant issues found in the system. These meetings usually start with **30 minutes** but can be extended if needed, depending on the complexity of the cases.

## What is it?
- A quality-focused meeting, scheduled as needed.  
- Presentation of bugs identified in production or during exploratory and automated testing.  
- May include detailed **log analysis** to better understand the issue.  

## Benefits ✨
- Keeps the team updated on the **system’s health**.  
- Ensures everyone understands how the system is used in production, highlighting the most critical cases.  
- **Decentralizes knowledge**, fostering collective learning.  
- In the medium and long term, helps the team gain deeper system understanding, perform more realistic testing, and improve quality metrics.  
- Enables smarter decisions about **what to automate**, since bugs often cluster around certain functionalities, guiding where to focus efforts.  

## How do we do it?⚙️
1. **Schedule short meetings** (up to 30 minutes).  
2. Define the time period for bug analysis (e.g., bugs from the past week).  
3. Focus primarily on **production bugs**.  
4. For each bug, we discuss:  
   - *Affected functionality*  
   - Explanation of the issue  
   - Priority level  
   - *Root cause analysis*  
   - Potential tests to cover the scenario  
   - Whether automation is worthwhile and how to implement it  
   - Insights and *action points*
